The better model
There should be three layers:
1. Internal workbooks
These are for Muloo only.
Use cases:
HubSpot audit notesInternal discovery checklistMeeting notes converted into questionsTechnical reviewScope planningRisk reviewDelivery assumptions
These should not show in the client portal unless explicitly shared.
2. Contributor workbooks
These are shared with specific people.
Use cases:
Tara answers commercial/project champion questionsGrant answers website/design questionsDevan answers tech stack/integration questionsFinance answers billing/process questionsOps answers service delivery questions
These people do not need full portal access.
They get a secure contributor link or invite. They only see the workbook/questions assigned to them. They answer, upload docs, add links, and then Muloo vets the input.
3. Client-facing approved view
This is what the client portal should show.
Use cases:
Progress summaryWhat input is still neededApproved discovery outputsHigh-level project planScope and quote statusDelivery progressMessagesTasks
The client portal should not show six raw internal workbooks by default. That is too noisy, and frankly it exposes the sausage factory. Clients do not need to see the sausage factory. They need the breakfast.
What is wrong in your screenshot
The client portal is showing a Workbooks tab, and it looks like the client can see long raw discovery workbook content.
That is dangerous from a UX point of view because:
It overwhelms the client.It makes the project look unfinished.It exposes internal structure too early.It blurs the line between contributor input and approved discovery.It turns the portal into admin homework.
For the client champion, this is okay only if the workbook is deliberately shared with them.
For normal client users, no.
For external contributors, definitely no full portal.
The correct visibility rules
Each workbook should have a visibility setting:
Internal onlyContributor link onlyClient champion visibleClient portal visiblePublished discovery outputArchived
And maybe a separate status:
DraftSharedIn progressSubmittedUnder reviewApprovedUsed in plan
That distinction matters.
Visibility answers: who can see it?
Status answers: where is it in the process?
The workflow should be this
1. Muloo creates workbook internally2. Muloo selects questions from the library or meeting notes3. Muloo assigns questions/sections to contributors4. Contributors answer via secure link, not full portal5. Muloo reviews/vets answers6. Approved answers become discovery evidence7. Discovery evidence feeds:   - project summary   - scope   - blueprint   - quote   - delivery plan   - risks and assumptions8. Client portal shows the approved/summary layer, not the messy raw layer
That is the product.
Right now it looks like:
Workbook created → dump into client portal
That is not the product.
